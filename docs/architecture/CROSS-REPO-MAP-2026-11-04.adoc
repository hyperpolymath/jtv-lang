= JtV language system: cross-repo map, proof and engineering ladders, critical path

Scope and conventions:

* *Sources.* The 16 repository maps supplied: jtv-lang (implementation), jtv-lang (design), jtv-halting-islands-ct, anytype, typell, typed-wasm, echo-types, epistemic-types, tropical-types, choreographic-types, occupancy-types, secret-types, residual-evidence-types, absolute-zero, januskey and maa-framework.
* *Status words.* "verified" means re-run in the mapping session. "Stated" means repo docs or CI only.
* *Missing toolchains in the mapping environment.* lean/lake/elan, agda, idris2, zig, just, wasm-pack, the wasm32 target, coqc, isabelle, julia and deno. Present: cargo/rustc, bun and node. The lead later installed the wasm32 target with `rustup target add` (2026-11-04); `cargo check -p jtv-core --target wasm32-unknown-unknown` then failed on source errors, not on the environment (see LANGUAGE-SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE.adoc H12). Every prover claim below is therefore stated-only unless marked otherwise.
* *Repos referenced but not mapped* (out of scope; nothing is claimed about their contents beyond what the maps cite): resource-algebra (pins inspected by the occupancy-types mapper only), patacl, systemet, affinescript, ephapax, 007, oblibeny, nextgen-typing, nextgen-languages, EchoTypes.jl, valence-shell, ochrance, aletheia-as-separate, hypatia, standards, cicd-suite, kategoria, ordinal-notation, typedqliser, lintel and wokelang.

== 2. Proof ladder

=== 2.1 Rungs

Each rung gives a status (LANDED / PARTIAL / OPEN), the prover and repo, and the trust boundary. "LANDED-stated" means the claim rests on repo docs or CI and was not re-checked here.

R0. Proof hygiene of the JtV suite: *PARTIAL (verified statically only)*.
* Lean 4 v4.12.0, jtv_proofs: 7 modules, 3,175 LOC.
* Verified by grep: 0 sorry/admit/axiom and 0 True-typed theorems.
* `lake build` was NOT re-run (no Lean toolchain). STATE/MATRIX claim exit 0 on 2026-06-21.
* The durable verifier is CI proof-regression.yml, whose run results were not inspected.

R1. Data core over the Int model: *LANDED-stated*.
* Lean: dataExpr_totality, data_terminates, data_is_pure, determinism, strong normalisation, confluence.
* Trust boundary: an Int-only denotational model (lit/var/add/neg over σ : String → Int).
* Caveat from the halting-islands map: in Lean, `∃ n, evalDataExpr e σ = n` is near-trivial, because definitions are total by construction. The real certificate is the termination checker accepting evalDataExpr.

R2. Injection/security over the Lean AST: *LANDED-stated*.
* no_control_to_data_flow and string_not_executable.
* Holds over the Lean DataExpr AST, not the pest grammar (MATRIX: parser_correctness absent).

R3. Per-op reversibility: *LANDED-stated*.
* rev_forward_backward (x ∉ freeVars e).
* Bennett token rev_forward_backward_with_token (JtvTheorems.lean:383–402).
* rev_backward_naive_fails_self_ref.
* End-to-end integration (gap-004) is OPEN. Rust has only 7 RV + 5 C2 tests.

R4. Echo/Epistemic lattice laws: *LANDED-stated, semantically thin*.
* blockEcho_admissible{,WithResidue}, injective_fibre_subsingleton, residue_lossy.
* Echo×Epistemic product-lattice laws.
* §6 NumSystem stratification is a table discharged by rfl/decide, so `float_not_safe` follows from the table, not from IEEE semantics.
* Epistemic in §5 is a 3-constructor enum with no semantics.

R5. type_preservation: *PARTIAL*. Holds for {int, hex, binary} under isIntRepr (gap-005).

R6. Number-system value models: *OPEN*.
* Rational and symbolic are designed in docs/proofs/number-system-value-models.adoc.
* Float and complex are Rust-witnessed only (number.rs approx_group_witness). Per do_not_reopen they are NOT to be Lean-proved via native_decide.

R7. Idris2 ABI: *PARTIAL, unchecked*.
* `%default total`; the GADT index makes CWhile : Expr DataLang ill-typed.
* dataIsNeverControl, purityLeqTrans and widenTrans are present.
* `dataAdditionOnly` is vacuous, and `DNeg` contradicts ADR-0008.
* idris2 is missing locally.

R8. Grammar-level injection impossibility and totality, i.e. PROOF-1 parser_correctness: *OPEN* (MATRIX: absent).
* The halting-islands map shows that once `data_expr` admits `function_call`, grammar membership is no longer a termination certificate. Totality then depends on the Pure Function Rule (typechecker.rs ~L680) plus call-graph well-foundedness.
* This rung needs three things:
** a grammar→AST conformance or round-trip statement;
** a theorem that Pure-annotated calls are well-founded (no recursion);
** the T1/T2/T3 statements from jtv-halting-islands-ct README/handover (membership ⇒ termination; diode one-way; islands contribute no non-termination).
* *Engineering soundness hole in front of this rung (doctrine 1):* `JtvWasm::run`/`run_and_collect` and `Interpreter::run` do not enforce purity. Any proof at this rung is bypassable on the embedding path until those entry points are fixed.

R9. Interpreter correctness, PROOF-2: *PARTIAL*.
* Exhaustive Rust testing at height ≤ 2 over lit/var/add/neg, i64 boundary.
* Missing: mechanised refinement; Control, reverse blocks, calls, lists, strings and the other number systems.
* Reusable pattern: residual-evidence-types `tests/correspondence`, which proves `checker-matches-explorer : table ≡ expected` by refl over 546 rows from a generated table. The JS half was verified (11/11 bun, byte-identical table). jtv already has a 7-point rfl-mirrored corpus to extend.

R10. Echo semantics anchored in echo-types: *OPEN*.
* echo-types holds the theory: EchoGraded (keep ≤ residue ≤ forget, ≤g-prop, degrade-compose), EchoLossTaxonomy (EQUIV/INJ/SURJ/CONST), EchoNoSectionGeneric and the Echo.* foundation contract. All LANDED-stated, CI green on 1073d60 (run 40446306048), not type-checked locally.
* Blockers:
** ADR-0007/0009 cite the retracted "graded comonad" framing. Sources, read locally at echo-types `1073d600004d13f1aec7363baa0ee2e38d91568e`: `docs/retractions.adoc` §R-2026-05-18, and `docs/echo-types/variance-resolution.adoc` with `proofs/agda/EchoVariance.agda` (landed as echo-types#243 per its CLAUDE.md). The Agda itself was not type-checked here.
** JtV's 3-point Echo fuses the loss-class axis and the retention-index axis, a fusion FOUNDATION_CONTRACT forbids.
** "Breaking = total erasure" has no fibre meaning: `const-fibre-section` shows the constant map's fibre keeps the whole domain.
** "tropical → Breaking" conflicts with EchoTropical (retains distinct echoes) and with FOUNDATIONS_BRIDGE §5 (tropical belongs on the resource axis).

R11. Token reversibility anchored to the family kernel: *OPEN*.
* Available pieces:
** echo-types `EchoReversibilityBridge.reversibility-via-totality : Σ B (Echo f) ↔ A` and `EchoResidueCell.once-only`, written for 007 (Agda, LANDED-stated);
** absolute-zero CNO.agda (IsCNO, cno-composition; LANDED-stated, unchecked).
* Gaps:
** absolute-zero's reversibility↔CNO bridge (reverses_seq_computes_identity, reversible_bridge_forward/backward_upto) exists only in ARCHIVED Coq.
** IsCNO's `terminates` and `cno-reversible` fields are vacuous (total eval; energy constant zero).
** Partial residues (float-carrier Neutral `x += 0.1` is not snapshotted in reversible.rs, so its reverse yields a candidate, not an identified pre-state) need residual-evidence-types vocabulary. Its DECISION-002 (family vs view) is unresolved.

R12. Epistemic semantics: *OPEN*.
* epistemic-types supplies Kripke/Frame.Box over Kripke/Residual.residualFrame, the factive/belief split, and de dicto vs de re (LANDED-stated, unchecked; no grade lattice).
* Two required corrections:
** ADR-0009 D3 "no cross-axis interaction" is likely false under fibre semantics: injective (Safe) outputs are Transparent.
** The Rust grader (epistemic.rs) under-reports in the unsafe direction: `x + 1` is graded Partial (pinned by a test), and If/While conditions are ignored, so implicit flows grade Opaque.

R13. Information-flow noninterference: *OPEN for JtV; source LANDED-stated in secret-types*.
* secret-types `Noninterference.Generic.tini` proves TINI for an addition-only lit/var/plus language under while/ifz with a pc label, generic over a 6-field minimal Lattice, with countermodels (N-8).
* CI cited at f149e08, not HEAD 7736da2.
* Gaps for JtV: no JtV bridge rule; no declassification (D154 requires it); TSNI OPEN; two-variable fragment only.
* jtv SECURITY_PROOFS.adoc §3 marks IFC "Verified" with an unchecked Lean tick and has no pc rule.

R14. Cost and occupancy grades: *OPEN for JtV*.
* Sources:
** resource-algebra (Agda kernel; not mapped);
** tropical-types `Resource.*` Lean (parametric_resource_transport) — provenance only;
** occupancy-types Reclaim.agda (reclaim-law, occupancy-at-cut). This is LANDED-stated but has NO CI lane: proofs.yml has no Agda job and check.sh has no Agda stage.
* Insight from the tropical map: tropical ⊗ is ordinary `+`, so JtV Data is a pure ⊠-fold and its halting island is a finite grade. The Isabelle Kleene star needs `no_pos_cycle`, and the Isabelle status is contested.

R15. Harvard bridge as a two-role choreography: *OPEN*.
* choreographic-types K-CUT-LOSS needs only ⊕-idempotence and ⊗-left-unit (LANDED-stated, unchecked). The JtV Echo join lattice would satisfy both.
* Missing: the "Data-never-selects" predicate. Recursion (μ-types) is OPEN, so Turing-complete Control cannot be modelled.

R16. Unified grade kernel: *OPEN*.

* typell levels are metadata-presence based (determine_safety_level), and LevelMonotonicity.SafeAt is vacuous.

R17. WASM compilation correctness, PROOF-3: *OPEN* (absent).
* typed-wasm side (stated):
** L1–L10 proofs erased by design;
** T4 layout equivalence and T5b WasmCert OPEN; the T5a Rust verifier is the trusted base;
** L15-C capability evidence is erased at emission (probe: 0 bytes of typedwasm.capabilities);
